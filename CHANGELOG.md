# Changelog

## 1.4.0 (2026-10-19)
- Combination products: the partner is a chemical of its own (weigh, dissolve, stock tables, diagram, PDF, CSV). Default preparation is two pre-primary stocks mixed into one Primary stock; parallel stocks remain available. Partner share of the Primary volume is user-set.
- New plate-centred layout view next to the flow diagram (with a section selector so it fits the screen).
- Inputs moved into movable floating windows opened from icons; diagram hotspots open the matching window.
- Small pictures beside every option and numeric field (role, combination convention, stock preparation, volumes, potency, series, inoculum).
- 60 automated tests (pre-primary mass balance, partner display results, separate mode).
